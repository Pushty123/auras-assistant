Terms & Permissions

The short version, in plain words.

  1. Your data is yours. What you share stays tied to you, and you can export or erase it at any time.
  2. Personal, not public. Aura learns your preferences only to shape its help for you, never to sell or profile you.
  3. You approve every action. Aura prepares calls, messages and tasks, but nothing is sent or done until you say yes.
  4. Permissions on demand. Microphone, notifications, contacts, calendar and location are requested only when you first use a feature that needs them.
  5. Helpful, not professional advice. Aura is not a doctor, lawyer or financial advisor. Check important decisions with a qualified person.
  6. Fair use. Use Aura for yourself, lawfully and kindly. Don't use it to harm, spam or deceive anyone.
  7. Terms can change. If these terms change, we'll show you what's new before you continue.
